How Quvirl Works

Quvirl is a real-time product demand intelligence radar built specifically for dropshippers, ecommerce entrepreneurs, and retail brands. We detect rising product demand from real search queries before markets become saturated.

The 4-Step Intelligence Workflow

📈

1. Real Google Trends Signals

We monitor live search queries across global markets to identify breakout volume spikes and sustained interest before competitors run ads on them.

🛒

2. Amazon Demand Validation

Curiosity is validated with real marketplace buying intent. We cross-reference Amazon bestseller search volume, buyer reviews, and pricing data.

🏭

3. Direct Supplier Matching

Every trending item is linked directly to vetted suppliers from CJdropshipping and AliExpress with accurate unit costs, real inventory, and shipping options.

💰

4. Live Profit & Margin Radar

Calculate realistic margins (typically 50%–75%), check break-even advertising costs, and sync winning products straight to your Shopify store.
